Privacy notice

Interim notice — awaiting Petals Health's policy text

This notice explains what happens to the details you give us when you use a form on this website (for example to book an appointment or ask for a call back). It is an interim notice written by the website team; Petals Health's full privacy policy will replace it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form: your name, mobile number and, if you choose to give them, your email address, gender, age, a preferred doctor, a preferred date and any message. We also record which page and form the request came from and the time it was sent.

Why we collect it

To contact you about the request you made — to confirm an appointment, call you back, or answer your question. We do not use these details for marketing unless you have separately agreed to that.

Where it goes

Your request is delivered to Petals Health's appointment team by email and stored in a secure spreadsheet that only the clinic's staff can open. It is not sold or shared with anyone outside Petals Health.

How long we keep it

For as long as is needed to deal with your request and your care, and as required by law for medical records.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time.
  • You can withdraw your consent by calling 9147405955 or by email (address to be added by Petals Health).
