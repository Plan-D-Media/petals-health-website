Reading it:
- **Every page pays 370 kB JS + 101 kB CSS + 200 kB font + 347 kB of logo** before anything paints. The logo is the
  1285×1627 PNG (264 kB) plus its 83 kB luminance mask, for a 46–65 px mark. LCP is ~4.1 s on text pages because the
  page is empty until the bundle runs. A real host gzips JS/CSS (~135 kB) but that still leaves the fonts and logo.
- **The doctor profiles are the outliers (7.1–7.5 s LCP)**: the LCP element is the washed background photograph,
  loaded at default priority after everything else; the hero text is painted at 4 s but the image is larger, so LCP
  waits for it. The profile pages also load 320–424 kB of fonts (Lora for the stat numbers, Playfair for monograms).
- **Image weight is the problem, and not only IVF**: Clinics (5.7 MB) is the worst by far because the Kankurgachi
  photo was copied at its 9.7-megapixel original; then Home, Find a Doctor and Yoga at 3.6–3.9 MB. Every treatment
  hero is a 1–1.4 MB PNG cut-out (alpha via a separate mask, so the PNG itself needn't be PNG). The doctor cut-outs
  (0.2–0.5 MB each) load on Home, Find a Doctor, IVF and the treatment team rows eagerly. IVF is mid-table: its
  illustrations were already downscaled; its cost is the hero PNG plus 15 requests.
- Find a Doctor's CLS appears once the filter panel is laid out under throttling; the shift sources are the facet
  labels inside `section.fd`. To be isolated during the fix.
- Home's hero video (1.27 MB) is lazy but still requested on mobile; the poster jpg is the LCP element at 5.2 s.

## C. Accessibility

Landmarks, labels, alt: `lang` set; header/nav/main/footer landmarks on every page **except Home, which has no
`<main>`** (the only page built before the template pattern). No page has a skip link; on mobile that is 3 stops,
on desktop 12–13 header stops before content on every page. Every `img` has alt; every input has a label; the dialog
is a native modal with an aria-label. Marquee duplicates are aria-hidden and inert.

Keyboard (real key presses, all 21 pages at 390 and 1366):
- Tab order follows the visual order on every page; every visible interactive element is reachable (closed footer
  accordions on mobile and the disabled "Previous" pager button are the only unreached items, both correct); every
  focused element shows the token ring; nothing hidden receives focus except the rating radios, which are visually
  replaced by their labels and show the ring via `:has()`.
- Treatments menu: ArrowDown opens and focuses the first item, arrows move, Escape closes and returns focus. Good.
- Form dialog: opens with focus on the first field, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger. Good.
- **Mobile drawer: Escape closes it but focus is dropped to `body` instead of returning to the menu button** (all
  pages). Focus is trapped correctly while open.
- **Rating radios cannot be cleared by keyboard** (see A5).

axe-core (390 and 1366, every page):
- **Contrast fails**: article date/meta text on Home, green `#00bf63` on white at 14 px = **2.43:1**; Find a Doctor
  filter labels with a zero count, grey `#808080` on white at 17 px = **3.95:1** (needs 4.5); the search placeholder
  uses the same grey. (axe also flags the sticky "Book Appointment" pill at 1.3–4:1 on ten pages; that is the pill
  sampled while it is `opacity: 0` and hidden. Its real ratio is 5.69:1. Noise.)
- **aria-label on non-role elements** (13 on Home, 1–3 elsewhere): the star rows in story cards, doctor cards and
  profile reviews carry `aria-label="5 out of 5 stars"` on a plain div/span, which screen readers ignore. Needs
  `role="img"`.
- **Carousel markup** (About team carousel): `role="group"` on `<li>` is not allowed and the `<ul>` track then has
  non-`li` children by role.
- **Heading order skips**: h1 → h3 on the clinic cards (Clinics page), doctor rows (Find a Doctor), and every
  Template A service card and the Multispecialty flagship tile (h3 directly under the page h1 with no h2).

Colour on things added since the token work: the purple filter/list borders (`#6e5fa1`, 5.5:1 on white) and the
navy tags on the profile are fine; the two failures above are the green meta text (pre-existing Home) and the
grey-500 used for "empty" filter options and placeholder text (new).

# Fix pass — 2026-09-19

## 1. Images and page weight

What changed
- `tools/img-measure.mjs` records the largest box every image renders in at 390/768/1366/1920; `tools/images.py`
  resizes each source to that box ×2 (capped at 1920 wide), bakes the luminance masks into the alpha channel (the
  eleven separate mask files and their CSS are gone), writes a WebP plus a JPEG/PNG fallback, an 800 px "-m" variant
  for phones, a 340 px "-sm" variant for doctor cards, and `src/generated/images.json` with every output size.
- `<Img>` (src/components/Img.jsx) renders `<picture>`: phone source, WebP source, fallback `<img>` with width/height
  from the manifest (space reserved before the bytes arrive), lazy by default, `priority` for the one LCP image.
- Per-route HTML from `tools/postbuild.mjs`: title/meta/canonical/OG, a viewport-split preload of the LCP image, and
  modulepreload/stylesheet links for the route's chunks. This replaced the meta-refresh stubs (which also fixes the
  lost-fragment problem in item 4).
- Routes are lazy chunks (React vendor 45 kB gz, shared 35 kB gz, page 1–5 kB gz); the form code loads on first open.
- Fonts: Bricolage variable 200 → 95 kB (unused `wdth` axis removed, Latin subset); Lora 82 → 32; Playfair 103 → 18.
- Hero video: desktop only (≥1024), attached after the window load event; phones get the poster.
- Logo: 1285×1627 PNG + mask (347 kB) → 131×166 PNG with alpha (21 kB / 11 kB WebP); favicon.png added.
