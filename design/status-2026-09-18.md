# Petals Health website — project note, 18 September 2026

## 1 · Home page: where it stands

**Built and verified.** The Home page is complete as a responsive website, not an artboard: it lays out correctly at
phone, tablet, laptop and desktop widths (390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1366 and 1920), and an automated check runs before
every commit to confirm nothing overflows, overlaps, clips or falls below the readable size and touch-target limits,
that the carousels and forms work, and that the page has no console errors. Everything below is on the page and
working: the header with its Treatments menu (drawer menu on phones), the full-width video hero with the headline card
and two booking buttons, the three family-care ribbons, the four-step process strip, the scrolling patient stories,
the scrolling doctor cards, the "Caring for All" photo block, the three article cards, the closing booking panel, the
footer, and the booking form that every button opens. Fonts load without the page jumping, and the page holds a
zero layout-shift score on a slow-phone test.

**Placeholder, marked as such on the page.** The hero video (a public-domain clip stands in). The six patient stories
(labelled "Placeholder" on each card). The seven doctors (from the design's Find-a-Doctor mock, with two source
errors flagged: a duplicated Dr Gutgutia with a man's photo, and a "12 am" timing). Doctor photographs (initials until
supplied). The main heading "Petals Health — Your Family Clinic in Kolkata" and the "Caring for All" block copy (both
tagged "Copy pending"). The booking form works on screen but sends nowhere yet. The privacy notice is an interim page.

**Blocked on the client.** Where form submissions go (the Apps Script address and the recipient email). The hero
footage (landscape 16:9; keep faces in the middle 70 % of the height). The doctor spreadsheet and photographs. Clinic
and lifestyle photography. Real patient stories with permission. The privacy policy text. The two pending wordings
and what "IWC" stands for. The tenth Treatments item and the contents of the Clinics and For Patients menus.
